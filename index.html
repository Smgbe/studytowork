A taste of what it covers

Germany and Canada work differently

The guide explains each rule in plain language and tells you what to confirm before you act. These examples were checked against official sources on 9 November 2026.

Germany

  • Job-search permit after your degree: up to 18 months.
  • Student work limit: 140 full days or 280 half days a year.

Canada

  • Off-campus work while studying: up to 24 hours a week.
  • Post-graduation work permit: up to 3 years for a master's started on or after 15 February 2024, if other conditions are met.

Sources: Make it in Germany, DAAD and IRCC. Rules change, so check the official sites before you decide.
