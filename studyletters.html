Honest limits

What it does not do

The samples are adapted from application drafts, with names, dates and grades removed. Use them as models and never copy them, because committees and officers notice repeated text. The pack does not promise an admission, a scholarship or a visa.

It covers two visa routes only: the Canadian study permit and the German Opportunity Card. It does not cover other countries' visas, other scholarship letters or Quebec rules. Rules and figures change, so check the official sites before you submit. This is general guidance, not legal or immigration advice.
